Additional rules:
- References keep their printed prefix: po_ref "PO-818", not "818"; bl_refs "BL-...".
- if_number is the number after "IF :", tp_number the number after "TP :", rc the number after "RC : <city>". They are different numbers; leave a field null rather than repeat another one.
- box: [x1, y1, x2, y2] on a 0..1000 scale is fine.
